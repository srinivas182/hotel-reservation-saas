# Assistant Hôtel — Your hotel answers on WhatsApp, day and night

> Your guests write on WhatsApp. The assistant answers in seconds, with **your** information, and prepares the bookings. **Your team always has the final say.**

## The problem

- Guests write at any hour: at night, during the morning rush, on Sundays.
- Every message not answered quickly is a booking that goes next door.
- Reception repeats the same answers a hundred times: prices, breakfast, parking, directions.

## The solution

| For your guests | For your team | For you, the owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An answer in seconds, in French or English, even at 2 am | Common questions already handled; booking requests ready to approve | More bookings without hiring; everything followed in one place |
| Voice notes understood | Arrivals, departures, room bill, restaurant, events on one screen | Every reply checked: supervised mode to start |

## What the platform does

- **WhatsApp assistant**: answers from your information pack, prepares the booking request, hands over to the team whenever needed.
- **Bookings and front desk**: availability and rates by category, room assignment, arrivals and departures, room bill and payments.
- **Restaurant and bar**: online menu with QR code, table bookings, room orders on WhatsApp.
- **Events**: spaces, quotes accepted online, deposits, event sheet.
- **Management**: roles per employee, two-step login, audit log, exports, daily backups.

## Built for West Africa

- Prices in FCFA and your currency; mobile money, card or transfer.
- Your country's VAT; invoices issued by our local partner, CNH Global Tech.
- French-first interface; online help, support Monday to Saturday.
- Each hotel's data is separate, encrypted and backed up daily.
