# Speed and reliability checks (S16)

| Check              | Where                                                      | Guarantees                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| No hidden queries  | `Model::preventLazyLoading()` (AppServiceProvider)         | Lazy loading fails in development and tests; in production it is only logged.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Query budgets      | `tests/Feature/Performance/QueryBudgetTest.php`            | Today, inbox, a conversation, bookings, a booking, the calendar, the orders board and the platform home stay within a fixed number of queries whatever the amount of data.                                                                                               |
| Indexes            | `2026_12_09_000100_add_speed_indexes.php`                  | WhatsApp notifications per number, audit log by event and date, subscriptions by status and period end.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Timeouts           | `tests/Feature/Reliability/ResilienceTest.php`             | Every outside HTTP call has a timeout; SMTP has a 15-second timeout.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Retries            | same                                                       | Every background job has a number of tries, a time limit, and back-off between tries; queued emails are retried 4 times (1, 5, 15 minutes).                                                                                                                              |
| AI circuit breaker | `App\Domains\Ai\Gateway\CircuitBreaker`                    | A model failing 3 times in 2 minutes is skipped for a minute (the next model answers); if every model fails, the conversation goes to staff; the model is tried again after a minute.                                                                                    |
| Outages            | `tests/Feature/Reliability/OutagesTest.php`                | Meta down: replies wait and are sent once. Email down: retried. Payment provider down: a clear message, nothing counted; a payment that cannot be confirmed stays pending and is counted once later.                                                                     |
| Load               | `tests/load/scale.js`, `.github/workflows/load-weekly.yml` | Weekly small run in CI (2 messages/s, 10 staff, 2 minutes); `SCALE=full` on staging (20 messages/s, 100 staff, 10 minutes). Targets: pages p95 under 500 ms on staging, webhooks under 1 s, under 1% errors, every conversation answered, no failed or duplicated sends. |

Local small run (simulated staging, PHP built-in server, 3 workers): 0% errors, pages p95 300–380 ms, webhooks p95 104 ms; 456 conversations, 456 answered, 0 failed sends, 0 duplicates.
